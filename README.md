## 🔍 About Me
Detail-oriented and curious about turning raw data into meaningful insights.  
I enjoy building data pipelines, exploring datasets, and creating dashboards that tell stories.  
My academic background and hands-on projects have given me a solid foundation in:

- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy) for data manipulation & analysis  
- **SQL** (SQLite) and **NoSQL** (MongoDB) for data storage & querying  
- **Power BI** & **Metabase** for interactive dashboards  
- **Linux**, **Git**, and **Docker** for reproducible development environments
